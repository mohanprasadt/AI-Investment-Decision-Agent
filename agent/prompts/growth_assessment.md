# Growth Assessment Research Template

## Purpose
Evaluate the quality, source, and sustainability of the company's growth. Not all growth is equal — organic growth from pricing power differs from acquisition-driven growth which differs from debt-funded growth.

## Research Prompt

**Ticker:** `{ticker}`
**Revenue CAGR (5yr):** `{revenue_cagr_5yr}`
**Revenue CAGR (10yr):** `{revenue_cagr_10yr}`
**EPS trend:** `{eps_trend}`

---

### Step 1 — Historical growth quality

- What has been the revenue growth rate over 3, 5, and 10 years?
- Has growth been consistent or lumpy?
- Has EPS growth kept pace with revenue growth? (if not, why not)
- Has FCF growth kept pace with earnings growth? (if not, why not — earnings quality check)

**Growth quality signals to investigate:**

| Signal | What to look for |
|--------|-----------------|
| Organic vs. acquired | What % of revenue growth came from acquisitions vs. same-store/organic growth? |
| Price vs. volume | Was growth driven by price increases (sustainable) or volume (may be cyclical)? |
| Share count | Has share count increased significantly? (dilutes EPS growth quality) |
| Working capital trends | Increasing DSO or inventory may signal channel stuffing |

---

### Step 2 — Growth drivers (forward-looking)

What will drive growth over the next 5–10 years?

**Identify the primary growth vectors:**
- Market expansion (new geographies, new customer segments)
- Product expansion (new products, cross-sell to existing customers)
- Pricing power (can the company grow revenue without growing volume?)
- Operational leverage (revenue growing faster than costs)
- Acquisitions (is M&A a repeatable, value-creating driver or a distraction?)

---

### Step 3 — Growth sustainability assessment

State clearly whether growth is sustainable and why:

- Is there a large, addressable, underpenetrated market?
- Is the growth runway long enough to matter?
- Are there headwinds that will compress growth rates?
- Is management's guidance plausible given historical execution?

**Label this paragraph as L2 (interpretation)** — clearly separated from L1 facts above.

---

### Step 4 — Risks to the growth thesis

- What would cause growth to decelerate significantly?
- Is current growth rate above long-term sustainable rate? (reversion risk)
- Does growth depend on macroeconomic conditions?
- Acquisition-driven growth: what if the acquisition pipeline dries up?

---

### Evidence requirements

- Historical growth rates should come from financial statements (Tier 1)
- Forward-looking assessments should be labeled as interpretation (L2) with stated assumptions
- If management has provided guidance, cite it but also assess its credibility

---

### What NOT to do

- Do NOT extrapolate past growth rates indefinitely into the future without justification
- Do NOT conflate revenue growth with earnings or FCF growth
- Do NOT assess valuation here (what growth justifies what multiple is Phase 5)
- Do NOT make investment recommendations
