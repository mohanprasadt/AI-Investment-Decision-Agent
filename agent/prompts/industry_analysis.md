# Industry Analysis Research Template

## Purpose
Assess the structural attractiveness of the industry the company operates in. A great company in a bad industry faces a constant headwind. This step precedes moat analysis — industry structure shapes what moats are possible.

## Research Prompt

**Ticker:** `{ticker}`
**Industry:** `{industry_name}`

---

### Step 1 — Competitive landscape

- Who are the main competitors?
- Is the market fragmented or concentrated?
- How intense is price competition? (commodity pricing vs. differentiated)
- What is the typical industry ROIC? (if available)

---

### Step 2 — Industry structure questions

Answer each:

**Pricing power environment:**
- Can companies in this industry raise prices above inflation? (Yes/No/Sometimes)
- What happens if one player raises prices — do others follow or does it lose share?

**Switching costs:**
- How easy is it for customers to switch to a competitor?
- Are there contractual, technical, or psychological lock-ins?

**Capital intensity:**
- Is this a high capex or low capex industry?
- Does high capex create barriers (moat-like) or destroy returns?

**Regulatory environment:**
- Are there meaningful regulatory barriers to entry?
- Is this industry subject to regulatory tail risk (price regulation, license revocation)?

**Technology disruption risk:**
- Is the industry's core product or service at risk of being disrupted by technology?
- What is the estimated timeline? (near-term <5yr, medium 5–10yr, long-term >10yr)

---

### Step 3 — Industry classification

Based on the above, classify the industry:

| Classification | Description |
|----------------|-------------|
| `ATTRACTIVE` | Pricing power, barriers to entry, rational competition, low disruption risk |
| `NEUTRAL` | Mixed characteristics — some favorable, some challenging |
| `CHALLENGING` | Intense competition, commodity pricing, high disruption risk, or regulatory overhang |

**State your reasoning explicitly.** Which factors drove the classification?

**Exception note (if applicable):** Even in a challenging industry, an exceptional company with a strong moat can be a great investment. If this applies, note it and flag for moat analysis to verify.

---

### Step 4 — Industry tailwinds and headwinds

**Tailwinds (industry-level):**
- List 2–4 structural tailwinds (demographic, regulatory, technology, etc.)

**Headwinds (industry-level):**
- List 2–4 structural headwinds

---

### Evidence standards for this section

- Structural facts (market share, capital intensity, switching costs) should come from Tier 1 or Tier 2 sources
- Opinions about competitive dynamics should be labeled as interpretations (L2), not facts (L1)
- Forecasts about disruption should state the source and confidence level

---

### What NOT to do

- Do NOT conflate company-level moat with industry-level attractiveness
- Do NOT assume an attractive industry means the company has a moat (it may not)
- Do NOT assume a challenging industry means the company cannot outperform
- Do NOT make valuation judgments here
