# Business Understanding Research Template

## Purpose
Establish a clear, honest baseline of what this business does, how it makes money, and whether it falls within the circle of competence. This is L1 fact-finding only — no moat claims, no valuation.

## Research Prompt

**Ticker:** `{ticker}`
**Company:** `{company_name}`
**Sector:** `{sector}`

---

### Step 1 — What does this business actually do?

Describe the core operations in plain language. Avoid jargon unless it is the company's jargon and you explain it.

- What product or service does it provide?
- Who are the customers (individuals, businesses, governments)?
- What geographies does it operate in?
- Is it one business or a portfolio of businesses?

**Output format:** 3–5 sentences, no bullet points. Plain language.

---

### Step 2 — How does it make money?

Identify the revenue model and profit mechanism:

- Primary revenue stream(s) — name them
- How does pricing work? (transaction, subscription, usage-based, spread, commission)
- Where does gross profit come from? (which segment, geography, product)
- What percentage of revenue flows to operating income and free cash flow?

If you don't know the answer with confidence, say so explicitly: "Revenue model is unclear from available sources."

---

### Step 4 — Understandability assessment

Answer these questions honestly:

1. Can I explain what this business does in one paragraph to a non-expert? (Yes/No)
2. Do I understand the key drivers of profitability? (Yes/No)
3. Do I understand what could go wrong? (Yes/No)
4. Are there any aspects I do not understand? (list them)

**Understandability rating:**
- `SUFFICIENT` — can explain business, profit model, and key risks
- `BORDERLINE` — understand the core but significant parts are opaque
- `INSUFFICIENT` — cannot reliably explain how the business makes money

If rating is INSUFFICIENT, stop here. Do not proceed with moat or valuation analysis.

---

### Evidence sources for this section

For each claim above, record:
- Fact (L1)
- Source (annual report, investor day, press release, etc.)
- Source tier (T1 = regulatory filing/annual report; T2 = reputable outlet; T3 = supporting)
- Date of source

---

### What NOT to do in this section

- Do NOT make any claim about competitive advantages here
- Do NOT assess valuation
- Do NOT assess management quality
- Do NOT make any investment recommendation
- If information is not available, say so — do not fill gaps with assumptions
