# Bull/Bear Case & Devil's Advocate Research Template

## Purpose
Force both sides of the argument. Mandatory devil's advocate research protects against confirmation bias. This is the most important guardrail in the research process.

## Critical Requirements (Non-Negotiable)

1. **Devil's advocate research is mandatory.** You must actively seek evidence that the investment thesis is WRONG before completing this section.
2. **Negative evidence must be logged.** Even if the bear case is ultimately dismissed, the evidence that informed the bear case must be recorded.
3. **Thesis breakers must be defined.** "What would make me change my mind?" must be answered with specific, observable events — not vague feelings.
4. **Confirmation bias check must be explicit.** State what you specifically did to avoid confirming your prior beliefs.

### Step 4 — Thesis Breakers

What specific, observable events would cause a complete reassessment of this investment thesis?

(Must be specific and observable — not "if things go badly")

Examples of good thesis breakers:
- "ROIC falls below 10% for two consecutive years"
- "Key customer (>30% of revenue) does not renew contract"
- "Management announces dilutive acquisition above $X billion"
- "Regulatory ruling removes the pricing power in [segment]"

### Step 5 — Confirmation Bias Check

**Explicitly state:**
- What did I believe about this company BEFORE the research?
- What did I specifically look for to challenge that belief?
- Did the research change my view in any way?
- What would make me NOT invest, regardless of moat or growth?

---

### Step 6 — Bull/Bear Summary

**On balance:** [One paragraph synthesis of the bull and bear cases, stating which evidence is stronger and why]

Do NOT conclude with an investment recommendation here. Conclude with:
"The bull case rests on [X]. The bear case rests on [Y]. The thesis is challenged by [Z]. The key question for valuation is whether [specific factor] is priced in."

---

### What NOT to do

- Do NOT skip the devil's advocate section because you are already convinced
- Do NOT dismiss bear case evidence without explaining why it doesn't change the thesis
- Do NOT make up thesis breakers — they must be grounded in actual risks identified
- Do NOT make a BUY/SELL/HOLD recommendation here
